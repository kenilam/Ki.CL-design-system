Frames a section that fills the window. It's inset by a gap on every side, with rounded corners, and its top follows the site header as it shows and hides. On load it eases from the window's full height into the frame, after `delay` milliseconds, and keeps its width throughout. It sizes to the window, so there's no preview here.

```
<Frame delay={300}>
  <section>…</section>
</Frame>
```
