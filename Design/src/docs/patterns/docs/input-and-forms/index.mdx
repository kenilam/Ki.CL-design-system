Forms are where people get things done, so every extra field or unclear label makes the job harder.

Build forms with the `Form` component. It renders a real `form`, so pressing Enter submits it. It turns off the browser's own validation pop-ups, which look different in every browser and vanish after a few seconds, and `FormMessage` shows each error under its field instead.

## Label every field

Every field gets a visible `label`, not just a placeholder. A placeholder disappears as soon as someone types, is usually too faint to pass contrast, and isn't a reliable name for assistive technology. Put hints in `FormDescription`, which the field points to with `aria-describedby`.

## Group what belongs together

Wrap related radios and checkboxes in a `fieldset` with a `legend`. `RadioGroup` and `CheckboxGroup` do this, so a screen reader announces the question when someone moves into the group.

## Ask for less

- Leave out anything you don't need. Each extra field is more work, and some people stop before the end.
- Use the right input type, so phones show the right keyboard and browsers can fill it in: `EmailInput`, `PasswordInput`, `type='tel'`, `inputMode='numeric'`.
- Set `autoComplete`, so returning people don't type their address again.
- Offer a choice instead of typing when the answers are known.
- Split a long form into steps, and let people save and come back.

## Errors

- Check a field when the person leaves it, not on every keystroke.
- Put the error under the field, in words that say how to fix it. `FormMessage` does this and links it to the field.
- Keep the submit button enabled. Pressing it shows what's missing; a disabled button just leaves people guessing why.
