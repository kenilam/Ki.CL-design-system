Shadows, frosted panes and scrims for things that sit over the page.

## Variables

### backdrop-filter

MDN: [`backdrop-filter`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/backdrop-filter).

The frosted pane behind the global header and anything else over a scrolling page.

<Variables
  names={['backdrop-blur', 'backdrop-filter']}
  property='backdrop-filter'
  preview={Backdrop}
/>

### scrim

The scrim over a picture, so text on it stays readable.

<Variables names={['scrim-opacity', 'scrim-top', 'scrim-foot']} />

## Classes

Shadows follow the pattern `kicl-box-shadow-{name}`.

```tsx
// The soft shadow under popovers and menus
<div className='kicl-box-shadow-overlay' />

// A frosted pane over a scrolling page
<header className='kicl-backdrop' />

// Darkens a picture so text on it reads
<figure className='kicl-position-relative kicl-scrim' />
```

`{name}` is `center`, `top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`, `overlay` or `none`. `overlay` is the soft shadow under popovers and menus.

- `kicl-backdrop` is the frosted pane: what's behind is blurred and tinted with `--kicl-color-page-a12`, so text on it stays readable. A pane over one element, like a spinner's, sets `--kicl-backdrop-tint` to a stronger one, such as `--kicl-color-page-a64`.
- `kicl-backdrop-blur` blurs what's behind less than `kicl-backdrop` does, and adds no tint.
- `kicl-scrim` mutes a picture under a wash of the page colour, from `--kicl-scrim-top` at the top to `--kicl-scrim-foot` at the foot. Put it on a positioned element.
