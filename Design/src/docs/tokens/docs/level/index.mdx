`level` says what something means, and each one has a colour: `neutral`, `confirm`, `error`, `info` and `warning`. `neutral` is black on a light page and white on a dark one, and a component with no level takes it. The same level is the same colour on Button, Badge, Card, Bubble, Heading, Text, Status, Markdown and Diagram. Status has no `neutral` or `confirm`.

Level and variant are separate props. A secondary error button fills with `--kicl-color-error-secondary`, and a card with a level only colours its edge. Each level has four looks: `secondary`, `tertiary` and `ghost` are steps `600`, `700` and `500` of its hue, and `ink`, for words on a tinted fill, is `800` on a light page and `200` on a dark one.

## Variables

`warning` mixes yellow and orange on a light page and is plain yellow on a dark one, and `neutral` is black or white. The others point at a hue.

## Classes

In a component, use the `level` prop, so the label, the fill and the edge stay in step. Outside one, the same colours apply with `kicl-color-{level}`, `kicl-background-color-{level}` and `kicl-fill-{level}`, where `{level}` is `neutral`, `confirm`, `error`, `info` or `warning`, with `-secondary`, `-tertiary`, `-ghost` or `-ink` for a look.

```tsx
// A card with its edge in the error colour
<Card level='error' />

// Text in the confirm colour, and anything inside that reads the body colour
<Text className='kicl-color-confirm'>Saved</Text>

// A warning fill behind a block
<div className='kicl-background-color-warning' />

// An SVG icon filled with the info colour
<svg className='kicl-fill-info' />
```
